# 5 Failure Articles — Analysis (2026-11-03)

User ke bheje 5 articles. Har ek ka verdict + workflow me kya kaam ayega.

## 1. Medium (The Sommie Way) — 7 mistakes: USEFUL (repeat + 1 naya)

7 me se 6 hamari pehli research repeat karte hain (validation, specific niche,
pain point, simple rakho, launch-and-disappear). **Naya mufid point:**
- **#5 Weak sales copy:** achha product bhi flop agar samajh na aaye — "What is
  this? Who is it for? What problem? Why care?" → **sales-page copy generator**
  ka gap (hamara caption pack social ke liye hai, sales page ke liye nahi).

## 2. Emvigo — product fit crisis: MIXED (2 portable ideas)

Enterprise-SaaS flavored, lekin 2 cheezen portable hain:
- **Sean Ellis test:** users se poocho "product na hota to kitne disappointed?"
  <40% "very disappointed" = no product-market fit. → **PMF survey automation**
  (post-purchase 1-question survey → scoreboard).
- **MoSCoW prioritization:** Must/Should/Could/Won't-Have — product scope
  discipline (ebook outline me "Won't-Have" column).
- Baaki (churn/NPS/retention metrics) hamare scoreboard me pehle se planned.

## 3. Nexway — challenges/benefits: SALES PITCH (2 real gaps)

Ye Merchant-of-Record bechne ka article hai. 2 asli gaps mile:
- **Piracy:** hamare PDFs ki **zero protection** hai — koi bhi forward kar de.
  → **PDF buyer-stamping** (har page par buyer email stamp, fpdf2 se — cheap,
  real deterrent).
- **Support expectations:** FAQ + clear delivery instructions → **auto-FAQ per
  product** (generator se).
- Free trials = hamara freebie funnel ✓ (already). Tax/payments = selling-layer
  decision (a) me jayega.

## 4. ITJ — life sciences: NOT USEFUL (honest)

Pharma/enterprise (FDA, EMA, clinical trials) — hamare business se **koi lena
dena nahi**. Skip. (Portable: QA rigor — hamare paas pehle se hai.)

## 5. Alva — 87% fail study (500 Shopify merchants): GOLDMINE (vendor bias ke saath)

**Bias flag:** ye delivery-infrastructure bechne wali company ka blog hai —
87% number dramatic marketing hai. Direction sahi, precision suspect. Phir bhi
data points kaam ke hain:

- **#1 Zero-validation (42% failures):** 10 logon ka purchase intent → success
  9% se 34% (4x). → hamara pre-sell gate ✓ (threshold 10 sahi hai!)
- **#2 Infrastructure collapse (31%):** download links expire, manual delivery
  scale par impossible. → **selling layer me secure expiring download links**
  (decision (a) ke spec me likh do).
- **#3 Pricing death spiral (28%):** <$27 → 84% fail; $97+ → 41% success.
  Sweet spots: **Templates $47-97, Ebooks $27-47.** Pricing guidance (user ka
  faisla, data-backed).
- **#4 Single-channel (24%):** multi-channel (3+) = **52% success** vs
  organic-social-only = 11%. Email foundation sabse stable (31%). →
  Marketing Machine validate hota hai.
- **#5 Support spiral (19%):** 3.7 tickets/10 sales; top issue "download nahi
  hua" (37%). → delivery automation + auto-FAQ.
- **#6 Commodity trap (17%):** Etsy par 47,000+ Canva sellers → differentiation
  zaroori → hamare edges (Hinglish, video, honest).
- **#7 Premature abandonment (14%):** profitability me avg **4.2 months**;
  71% ne month 3 se pehle chhod diya. → daily scheduler + sabar.
- **Winner's playbook:** 92% ne 50+ engaged subscribers ke SAATH launch kiya.

### 4-Level Validation Ladder (user ke "90% bharosa" ka formula)

1. **Problem validation:** 30 din me 100+ log problem ki shikayat karein
   → Problem Miner ka metric
2. **Solution validation:** 10 beta users, 70%+ satisfaction
   → pre-sell gate + Sean Ellis survey
3. **Channel validation:** 3 channels test, 1 profitable
   → Marketing Machine router
4. **Systems validation:** delivery automated, <2 tickets/10 sales
   → selling layer

Har level agle ka gate hai. Ye ladder = 90% ke sabse kareeb ka system.
