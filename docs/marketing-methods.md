# Marketing methods for digital products

Analysis date: 2026-11-12. Channels verified live the same day via search
(secondary guides + platform pages quoted in results — re-check each
program on its own official page at setup time; these programs change
often). Products in scope: OWN digital products (ebooks, template packs)
+ POD designs. Affiliate products are other brands — they are NOT
claimable as own products on any of these surfaces.

## Seedhi baat: 11 naam, 5 kaam

GEO / AEO / LLMO / AIO chaaron ek hi kaam hain (AI answers mein cite
hona). VSO usi ka spoken version hai. Baaki: SEO (foundation), SMO
(social), SXO + CRO (store live ke baad ka optimization). ASO aur LSO
hamare case mein lagte hi nahi.

## Har method ka verdict

- **SEO** — CHAHIYE, foundation. Store live ke baad: product pages +
  Product/FAQ schema + guides/articles. Abhi BLOCKED (store live nahi).
  Etsy SEO pehle se built hai (title + 13 tags optimizer).
- **GEO + AEO + LLMO + AIO** — EK hi kaam: AI answers mein product
  cite ho. Kaam: buying guides, comparisons, Q&A pages, structured
  data, real reviews. Chaar alag build nahi.
- **VSO** — voice search: AEO content hi hai, bas conversational
  phrasing ("best budget planner kaunsa hai"). Alag build nahi, low
  priority.
- **SXO** — SEO + UX (fast page, clear design, easy checkout). Store
  live ke baad.
- **CRO** — selling live + traffic ke baad. Goal-loop/flywheel pehle se
  metrics measure karta hai — CRO usi ka optimization hai.
- **ASO** — apps ke liye hai. Hamari koi app nahi. NOT APPLICABLE, skip.
- **LSO** — local business ("near me") ke liye. Digital products
  global hain, koi dukaan nahi. NOT APPLICABLE, skip.
- **SMO** — sabse strong lane, PEHLE SE BUILT: IG/FB publishing,
  Pinterest/LinkedIn/X multi-publish, campaigns, repost loop, auto-DM,
  best-time gate. Agla step: product tagging (Meta Shops) + Pinterest
  catalog, store live ke baad.

## ChatGPT Business Hub jaisi aur surfaces (verified 2026-11-12)

### Ek feed, paanch surfaces — sabse badi baat

Merchant Center format ka ek product feed banao to Google AI Mode,
Perplexity, Pinterest aur Meta — sab wahi feed padhte hain. Alag-alag
integration nahi, **ek feed endpoint** (backend catalog → Merchant
Center format). Wahi feed ChatGPT discovery ko bhi crawl se milta hai.

### 1. Google Merchant Center — sabse bada

- Free listings India mein available hain. Products aa sakte hain:
  Shopping tab, Search, Images, Lens, YouTube, Maps, Gemini, AI Mode.
- India mein AI Mode shopping answers mein price, reviews aur
  inventory dikhte hain; Gemini app mein listings English aur Hindi
  dono mein aati hain.
- Merchant Center mein AI performance report hai jo India accounts ke
  liye AI Mode / AI Overviews mein share of voice batata hai.
- Chahiye: verified website + product feed (ID, title, description,
  link, image, price, availability, brand, GTIN jahan applicable).
- CAVEAT (verify at setup): ebook/template jaise digital products ki
  Merchant Center eligibility confirm nahi ki — setup time check karni
  hogi. POD physical products pakka fit hain.

### 2. Pinterest Catalogs — visual products ke liye best

- Free Business account, free catalog, free organic product pins.
- Pinterest ka claim: catalogs waale merchants ko 5x zyada impressions
  (unka apna claim, methodology nahi — directional samjho).
- 640M monthly active users (June 30, 2026). Templates/planners jaise
  visual products ke liye best visual search engine.
- Chahiye: claimed domain + Pinterest tag + feed.
- Etsy nuance: Etsy shop claim nahi hoti aur catalog nahi banta — lekin
  Etsy listings ko product Rich Pins AUTOMATICALLY mil jate hain, bina
  setup ke.

### 3. Meta Shops (Instagram + Facebook)

- Commerce Manager mein free setup; catalog free.
- Sept 2025 se shops website checkout par hain — Meta payment process
  nahi karta, to Meta koi cut nahi leta; sirf tumhara payment
  processor charge karta hai.
- Ek photo post mein 20 products tag, story sticker mein 5.
- Yahi catalog IG + FB + WhatsApp (neeche) teenon ko feed karta hai.

### 4. WhatsApp Business catalog — India ka sabse direct surface

- Free app, free catalog, 500 items tak; customer chat ke andar
  browse karta hai, cart bhejta hai, tum checkout link dete ho.
- Ye catalog Meta Commerce Manager wale catalog se connect ho jata
  hai — dobara type nahi karna.
- API waala version paid hai (India mein marketing message ~₹0.88);
  app waala free hi kaafi hai shuru mein.

### 5. Perplexity Merchant Program — digital ke liye galat fit

- Free, zero commission, approval 3–7 business days, feed Google
  Shopping CSV format (SFTP/API/S3).
- LEKIN: "physical goods ke liye optimized hai, digital products ke
  liye designed nahi"; GTIN mandatory; Buy with Pro checkout US-only.
- Digital ke liye yahan GEO play hai: citable guides likho,
  Perplexity cite karega — feed nahi. POD physical products baad mein
  qualify kar sakte hain.

### 6. Product Hunt — famous lekin galat fit

- Free to post (personal account, kam se kam 1 week purana).
- LEKIN templates, boilerplates, courses, books aur stores kabhi
  feature nahi hote — hamare template packs/ebooks is list mein hain.
- Sahi venues: **Uneed** (free line) aur **Peerlist Launchpad** —
  dono templates accept karte hain. Launch tab jab store ya koi tool
  launch ho, template pack par nahi.

### 7. Microsoft Copilot Checkout — emerging

- Wahi feed fields maangta hai, abhi early hai. Feed banne ke baad
  verify karenge.

### Pehle se jaana hua (2026-11-05 + 2026-11-12)

- **ChatGPT Business Hub** (beta, launched 2026-11-10): claim free at
  launch, product feed + insights; Business Agent US-only; India =
  claim + feed + insights only. Sequence: store live → selling unpark
  → claim + feed → insights watch. Placement/sales ki koi guarantee
  nahi (OpenAI ke apne words).
- **Marketplaces with built-in traffic:** Etsy (built, SEO optimizer
  included), Creative Market, Canva Creators, Merch by Amazon — ye
  templates ke discovery engines hain. Gumroad/Payhip/own website =
  self-driven (seller ko traffic laana padta hai).

## Blocker + sequence

Sabka ek hi gate hai: **store live + selling unpark**. Uske baad:

1. Ek feed endpoint (backend catalog → Merchant Center format).
2. Submit: Merchant Center + Pinterest catalog + Meta catalog +
   ChatGPT Business Hub claim.
3. GEO guides likho (buying guides, comparisons, Q&A).
4. Har launch par Uneed / Peerlist.

## Rules (non-negotiable)

- Koi bhi method viral ki guarantee nahi deta — ye distribution hain.
  Viral tab hota hai jab shareable content + itni distribution +
  flywheel jo bataye kaunsa channel kaam kar raha hai.
- Reviews sirf REAL (Etsy buyer reviews). Fake testimonials,
  fake urgency, unverified income claims — Shariah rule ke against,
  kabhi nahi.
- Prices feed aur store mein hote hain (wahan zaroori hain) — generated
  videos/captions mein prices kabhi nahi (standing rule).
